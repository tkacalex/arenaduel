# ArenaDuel V1 Game Design

## Core

Competitive one versus one first person fantasy movement shooter.

## Match

1. First to 5 round wins
2. 90 second standard rounds
3. Maximum 9 rounds
4. No respawn during an active round
5. Future overtime objective when the timer expires

## Core skills

1. Aim
2. Movement
3. Positioning
4. Decision making
5. Utility usage
6. Weapon selection

## Fighters

1. Shadow
2. Warden
3. Rift

All fighters use the same weapon pool. Their future differences are primarily utility and movement abilities.

## Weapons

1. Arc Rifle
2. Shade SMG
3. Rune DMR
4. Hex Shotgun

All players have equal access to the weapon pool. Weapons are not permanently stat gated.

## Ability rule

V1 abilities do not directly damage opponents.

## Movement target

Sprint, slide, slide jump, mantle, vault, wall run, wall jump, and air control are planned future movement features. The current phase implements only walk, look, and jump.

## RPG

Match based upgrade choices.

## Style

Arcane dark fantasy.

## UI

Competitive minimal fantasy.
